A failed row is retried on the next run, not inside the same one, and never after its 5th attempt. The offer is tagged `offer_<slug>` because a visitor chooses its text, and a bare slug of `consent_sms` would otherwise switch on texting. A failed lead send is retried after 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours, then stops at 5 attempts. **No webhook configured**: nothing is sent or queued, agents stay `pending` and leads stay `pending`, and both Agent Finder list responses (`GET /agent-finder/runs`, `GET /agent-finder/agents`) now carry `delivery` with `configured` false and `message` "HighLevel webhook not configured", plus agent and lead counts by delivery status, `exhausted` (failed and out of attempts), `maxAttempts` and the last pass. Setting the webhook does not send the backlog by itself: the next sync run does, or the retry route. **`POST /xophz-freshmints/v1/crm/retry-deliveries`** (`manage_options`; anonymous 401, a user without it 403) runs one pass for agents and one for leads now, and answers `{ agents: { sent, failed, stopped, remaining }, leads: { delivered, failed, remaining }, delivery }`; with `reset_attempts` true it first gives failed agents and leads their attempts back, which is the only way past the cap. With no webhook it answers `freshmints_crm_webhook_not_configured` (400). The store gains `deliverable_agents()`, `count_deliverable()`, `delivery_counts()` and `reset_failed_attempts()`. Filters: `freshmints_crm_delivery_use_action_scheduler`, `freshmints_crm_delivery_time_budget`, `freshmints_crm_delivery_pause`, `freshmints_agent_finder_delivery_summary`. Options: `freshmints_crm_delivery_last` (last pass: time, sent, failed, why it stopped) and the lock `freshmints_crm_delivery_lock`.
